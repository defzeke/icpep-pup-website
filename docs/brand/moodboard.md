| Reference | Link | What to take from it |

| Tendril Studio — Maxon | <https://tendril.studio/work/maxon/> | Dimensional product rendering; how a technical object is lit so it reads as premium rather than diagrammatic |

Tendril Studio is a motion/3D studio, so these references are about **dimensional
rendering and lighting**, not layout. Read them as "how should our surfaces behave", not
"how should our pages be arranged".

## How this maps to the theme

Both references sit directly behind the **Atmospheric** pillar: deep, near-black bases, and
light that appears to be emitted from inside a form. That is the same treatment as the
hero logo variant (`public/logos/icpep-hero-glow.png`), where a soft halo rises from the
top layer of the diamond stack.

The deck's own cover art (page `01`) applies the same idea — so when in doubt, the cover is
your most reliable in-house reference.
