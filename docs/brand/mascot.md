# Pixie — Official Mascot

Source: deck page `10` (*ID // PIXIE · ROLE // OFFICIAL MASCOT*). Assets are in the repo
under `public/mascot/`.

## Identity

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Name | **Pixie** |
| Role | Official mascot |
| Assets | `pixie-model-sheet.png` (1920 × 1080), `pixie-illustration.png` (1229 × 1229) |

The deck presents Pixie in the terminal-key style from the **Algorithmic** pillar —
`ID // PIXIE`, `ROLE // OFFICIAL MASCOT` — which is worth reusing as the caption pattern
wherever Pixie appears on the site.

## Design

A humanoid character, anime-influenced, drawn in a loose digital-paint style:

- **Headset** — large black headphones worn over the head; the right cup is marked with a
  **white star**, the same star as the logo mark.
- **Horns** — two short pale horns emerging through the headset band.
- **Hair** — black, with the fringe and a tied mass reading as a ponytail, tipped in
  red-orange. The back/side view shows a dark backpack.
- **Outfit** — deep red sleeveless garment with an orange neck ribbon, a ribbed trim at the
  hem, black gloves, black shorts with a bright orange panel, and chunky black-and-orange
  boots.
- **Face** — a single large orange eye, orange cheek blush, and a small frown-like mouth.

The palette on the model sheet maps directly onto the brand: black/obsidian, orange
(`#F45A0C`–`#FB7D14`), maroon/crimson (`#7F0525`–`#CB0E37`), plus a pink-ish highlight —
i.e. Pixie is *drawn from* the palette rather than sitting beside it.

## The two assets do different jobs

| File | What it is | Use it for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `pixie-model-sheet.png` | **Reference sheet** on white: front view, side view, two chibi heads, three expression studies, and a paint palette strip | Designers and illustrators only. Not a page-ready asset — it has a white background and reads as a sketch. |
| `pixie-illustration.png` | **Finished artwork** on white: one dynamic full-body pose, reaching forward | The one to use on the site |

## Expressions available

From the model sheet, three researched emotions exist:

1. **Cross / annoyed** — narrowed eye, flat brows, small frown.
2. **Delighted** — crescent closed eyes, star-shaped sparkles in the eyes, wide smile.
3. **Flustered** — wide eyes, heavy blush, open mouth.

All three are strokes on the sheet, not finished assets. Using an expression on the site
means commissioning a finished version in the illustration's style.

## Usage (proposed)

The deck gives no mascot rules, so these are proposals pending the graphics team:

- Treat Pixie as a **character, not a logo** — never place her inside the logo lockup or
  replace the mark with her.
- Keep the artwork **uncropped**; the pose is designed to be read as a whole.
- **Size sparingly.** Pixie is a full-colour, high-detail illustration; on a dark
  Obsidian background she needs a container or a knockout treatment, and she should not
  fight the hero logo for attention.
- **Scale down, don't up.** Both files are rasters; enlarging past their native size will
  soften the line work.
- Do not recolour the outfit, remove the star from the headset, or redraw the face.

## Where Pixie belongs

Candidate placements, in order of how well they fit the brand:

1. **CTA / Join Us** — a friendly figure on the one section asking the visitor to act.
2. **About** — paired with the vision statement, humanising a technical paragraph.
3. **Empty / error states** — a memorable 404 is a genuinely good use of a mascot.
4. **Events** — only if event art already uses her; otherwise she competes with event branding.

She is a poor fit for: the header (too detailed at nav scale), the footer (too playful for a
utility area), and any data-dense section such as Projects or Executives & Advisers.

## Open questions

1. **Is Pixie's finished art a raster export only?** A vector or layered source would let
   the site use a transparent-background version — the current file has a white background,
   which is awkward on Obsidian.
2. Who is the **artist of record**, and who may commission new poses?
3. Which **expression** is canonical for the site, and is a finished version available?
4. Is there an approved **transparent** cutout of the illustration?
5. Is Pixie allowed in **photographic or social contexts** outside the website?
6. Is there a **pronoun/gender** or a one-line character description for alt text?
